37. Final Product Definition
If I had to describe the entire product in one sentence:
The Digital Museum is an immersive personal portfolio where a developer's projects, experiments, skills, and journey are presented as a curated collection of digital artifacts inside a futuristic virtual museum.
And if I were writing the product principle on the wall of the team room, it'd be:
â€œMake them curious enough to enter, impressed enough to explore, and informed enough to remember.â€
That should be the north star.
The biggest strategic recommendation I'd make is not to start coding the 3D museum yet. First lock the MVP information architecture + user flow + content model + visual/design system. Once those are solid, the 3D layer can be added as progressive enhancement rather than becoming the thing that dictates the entire product. That approach also aligns with real museum-web projects that prioritize clear visitor journeys, responsive design, accessibility, and reusable content structures alongside immersive presentation.
